## Brand Voice Rules — Copy Writing Guide

**Words to AVOID:**
- swipe, match, premium, boost, algorithm, AI-powered, ELO, score, tier, upgrade, subscription

**Words to USE:**
- connect, meet, find, genuine, real, free, human, community, verified, authentic, story, together

**Tone:**
- Direct and confident — not apologetic
- Slightly indignant about the dating industry — calls out what's wrong with competitors
- Warm but not saccharine
- Never sounds like a sales pitch

**Key Brand Messages (repeat throughout):**
1. "100% free — forever"
2. "No paywalls"
3. "Human-verified" (not AI, not automated)
4. "AI-free" / "Algorithm-free"
5. "Real people, real connections"
